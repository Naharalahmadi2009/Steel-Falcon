# STEEL FALCON

A 2D top-down tactical rescue game made for Jam 96.

## How to play

**Goal:** Rescue your teammate, survive the enemies, and reach the yellow EXTRACTION zone on the right side of the map.

### Controls

| Action | Key |
|---|---|
| Move | WASD or arrow keys |
| Sprint | Hold Shift |
| Crouch / sneak | Hold C |
| Aim | Move the mouse |
| Shoot | Hold left mouse button |
| Reload | Press R |
| Rescue teammate | Press E when close |
| Restart | Refresh the page after mission ends |

### Step-by-step mission

1. Start in the bottom-left corner as the blue soldier.
2. Move toward the green **RESCUE** marker.
3. Stand close to the teammate and press **E**.
4. Pick up yellow weapon pickups to change weapons and ammo.
5. Aim with the mouse and hold the left mouse button to shoot enemies.
6. Hold **C** to crouch and avoid alerting enemies as easily.
7. Hold **Shift** to sprint, but watch your stamina bar.
8. After rescuing the teammate, move together to the yellow area labelled **EXTRACT** on the far right.
9. Reach the extraction zone to complete the mission.

### HUD

- **HP:** Your health. If it reaches zero, the mission fails.
- **SPRINT:** Your stamina. It refills when you stop sprinting.
- **AMMO:** Your current weapon and ammunition.
- **TEAM:** Shows whether the teammate has been rescued.
